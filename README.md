I made these guides from my own experience, including my mistakes, so you don't have to
make the same ones. They're written for people who have never used an AI tool before, and
they take you step by step to real, useful work.

**The easiest way to read them:** [hans-tandt.github.io/AI-Guides](https://hans-tandt.github.io/AI-Guides/).
Pick your language and download the PDF.

## Good to know

- **AI tools change fast.** These guides describe Claude as of **August–September 2026**. If a screen,
  menu or model name looks different, trust the app. The habits and the way of working
  stay valid.
- **Editable versions.** The Word files are in [`source/`](source/), one folder per language.
  Use them to translate a guide or adapt it for your team.
- **Found a mistake or something out of date?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/Hans-Tandt/AI-Guides/issues).
